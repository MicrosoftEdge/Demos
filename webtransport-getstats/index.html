These are the spec's statistics, including fields your browser may not return. Returned includes measured 0 and false; Returned (null) means no estimate. Not returned means the field is absent, not necessarily unimplemented: it may be temporarily unavailable or withheld for privacy. Stats refresh about one second after sending a datagram, or when you click Refresh stats. The datagram counters are from the browser; “Datagrams echoed” is counted by this page.
